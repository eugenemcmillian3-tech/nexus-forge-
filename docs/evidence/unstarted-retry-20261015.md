# Fenced retry of unstarted Council work — 2026-10-15

Mission Control offers a distinct Authorize unstarted retry decision only when the server finds an uncertain runner Council claim, a paused execution_recovery run within its original deadline and retry allowance, an approved active mission, and no newer run. The latest portfolio decision must not conflict, and any dependency/resource requirements require separate preflight review. There must be no provider invocation for that runner, no Council effect or saved context under the claim, no mission artifacts/deliveries, no escalated or oversized incident history, and no other running/uncertain Council or tick claim. A prior hold/escalate review alone does not count as execution work. Absence checks are specific indexed ranges or bounded checks that fail closed.

The authorized mutation rechecks ownership, evidence freshness and every condition in one transaction. It retires only the exact old claim ID, consumes one existing retry, preserves the original deadline, records an audit and schedules the existing runner. No approval, budget, delivery or verification gate is bypassed. This decision can lead to provider inference when the scheduled runner rechecks prerequisites; it is separate from Keep blocked and Escalate.

Provider admission now checks the exact active Council claim in the same transaction as reservation. The guarded Council action attaches its claim ID to recovery.reserve and usage.begin calls. Runner Council stages without a token fail closed, including older in-flight workers that do not supply one. Supplied tokens also require mission approval/status, runner binding/status and deadline. Claim IDs are not reused. A delayed old worker cannot reserve inference after retirement even if a replacement claim has been acquired. Existing Council side-effect fences reject its late writes. Provider admission before retirement makes the absence proof fail; retirement before admission makes the old token fail.

No provider ledger is cleared, no completed result is discarded and no uncertain tick claim is released. Legacy/direct claims, recorded inference, saved inputs/effects and later handoffs stay blocked for reconciliation. Retired claim references remain in review/audit receipts for investigation. There is no automatic age-based expiry or general resume.
