Verification & Acceptance Checks

  • Every checklist item requires one explicit answer: Yes, No, or Needs Review.
  • Score is computed on a 100-point scale (10 items × 10 points each).
  • Yes = 10 pts, Needs Review = 5 pts, No = 0 pts per item.
  • Validation blocks score calculation until all 10 items are answered, with a visible error message.
  • Readiness classification bands: Ready (85–100), Conditionally Ready (60–84), Needs Review (35–59), Not Ready (0–34).
  • Breakdown panel shows count of Yes / No / Needs Review answers used in scoring.
  • Reset control clears all answers and hides prior results.
  • Layout is responsive: items and controls stack vertically below 600px viewport width.
  • No external dependencies, network calls, or deployment — fully self-contained static file.
